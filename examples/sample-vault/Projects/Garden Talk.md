# Talk: Soil as Infrastructure

Working title for the Maple City Dev Days submission (see [[Conferences]]).

## Idea
Treat compost, mulch and drainage like an engineering stack: you design for failure, you measure, and you
iterate. Developers in the audience already think this way about servers.

## Outline
1. The balcony that failed: what went wrong in 2024 (waterlogged pots, one dead lemon tree).
2. Measure first: a 12 euro moisture sensor and a spreadsheet.
3. Compost as a pipeline: inputs, processing time, outputs, and how to debug a smelly bin.
4. What the Elm Street garden taught me about volunteers and on-call rotas.
5. Takeaways and a seed giveaway.
